## Shape decision (parent left grouped-vs-split to the fact pass)

Two crisp notes, not one grouped card: the two risks are different organ systems with different
mechanisms (musculoskeletal injury from PULLING vs integumentary injury from GRIPPING) — two
topics per Layer A #1. The pulling/gripping verb pair is the natural discriminator, so each card
is cued by its own verb and a `Distinguish:` cross-link pins the pair (recipes §8 pattern).

- **P1** — causal fact, grouped pair under c1 with category slot-labels (`::joint injury` /
  `::bone injury`) to kill open-set drift ("pain"? "skin tear"?) and interference with P2.
- **P2** — property→consequence, two-way-ish: c1 = the skin property (forced binary
  thicker/thinner), c2 = the consequence pair (tears + bruising). Each direction cold-solves:
  c1 card is cued by the consequences, c2 card by the mechanism (thin skin).
