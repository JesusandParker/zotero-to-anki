           Transcriptional Initiation
• Binding of RNA polymerase
   – E. coli: Holoenzyme contains 5 subunits
   – α2 ββ' σ (480 kDa)
   – α subunits are involved in assembly
   – β subunit contains rNTP binding site
   – β' subunit contains DNA template binding region
   – σ subunit recognizes promoter sites in DNA

• “Melting of DNA”

• Chain synthesis: 2-9 nt (abortive < 10 nt)
