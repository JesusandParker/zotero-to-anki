       Removal of Intron Sequences by RNA Splicing
       The noncoding introns are excised from gene                               Researchers have shown that introns are removed from RNA sequences in
                                                                                 different ways. The remaining RNA sequences are then joined together.
       transcripts by several different mechanisms.                              The entire process is referred to as splicing. Three splicing mechanisms
                                                                     have been studied in considerable detail:
                                                                     1. The introns of tRNA precursors are excised by precise endonucleolytic cleavage
                                                                        and ligation reactions catalyzed by special splicing endonuclease and ligase activities.
                                                                     2. The introns of some rRNA precursors are removed autocatalytically in a unique
                                                                        reaction mediated by the RNA molecule itself. No protein enzymatic activity is
                                                                        involved in this splicing process.
                                                                     3. The introns of nuclear pre-mRNAs (hnRNAs) are removed in two-step reactions
                                                                        carried out by complex ribonucleoprotein particles called splicesomes. These splic-
                                                                        ing organelles are composed of RNA and protein, and are found in the nuclei of
                                                                        eukaryotic cells.

                                                                     discuss the sequences involved in guaranteeing accuracy in the splicing process.
