## Things she said worth putting on cards

- **kasra-after-alif (imaala):** 'this is the case where we turn the [aa] into [an e] sound because we have the kasra under the letter after alif' (26:33). Plain waajib is 'not wrong, but that's not how natives say it' (27:29).
- **sukuun makes a new syllable:** bay-tii / jay-bii, even when the sukuun is not written; three correct ways to vowel baytii and khawkh (fatHa only, sukuun only, both).
- **waaw/yaa consonant vs vowel:** wujuub - 'the first waaw is a consonant, the second is a vowel'.
- **Read without tashkiil:** 'hopefully in the future you would not even need the fatHa' (10:55); jiibuutii 'does not need any tashkiil because we have vowels' (18:53).
- **bi-khayr vs khayr:** bi-khayr is THE answer to 'how are you'; khayr / al-khayr is 'good' in general (41:09-41:35).
- **Gender:** women must say ana jayyida; Habiibatii only for a fiancee/girlfriend - not a friend of the opposite sex (stricter than the book).
- **Register pairs she ran:** waajib (fuSHa) vs waZiife (Shami); bi-khayr vs mniiH/mniiHa; tamaam and maashi are dialect.
- **No verb 'is':** 'we don't have is in Arabic' (42:02) - haadhaa kitaab, haadhaa waajibii.
- **taa marbuuTa -> t before a possessive:** waZiifa -> waZiifatii (43:30).
- **haadhaa / haadhihi pronunciation** gets class time: her 201 students still get it wrong (42:18).
- **Culture:** Arabic greetings are 'more intentional' than 'what's up' - you stop and talk (46:44).
