## Her key coaching lines (for card backs)

- **Haa**: epiglottis; no vibration, air forced through a slightly closed throat; tongue a little back and relaxed; fog a mirror; the 'Haa' over hot sweet tea; 'like going to the gym'. She exaggerates it on purpose for listening practice.
- **naHla / nakhla / nahla** = bee / palm tree / a girl's name. 'If you're saying nakhla you're hitting above where you're supposed to hit.'
- **khaa**: 'like clearing your throat'; soft palate, 'back of your throat, at the top'; 'with a khaa there is some little bit of vibration, but up'.
- **taHt / takht** = under (required) / bed (optional): 'same tashkiil, same letters, but the dots is the difference'.
- **Stacking**: she stacks the next letter on top instead of the book's climb-from-the-line; push the dot toward the right so it belongs to the first letter.
- **Habiibi** is fine with children or between spouses/couples, 'inappropriate in a formal setting'; Habiibti to a woman.
- **khawkh**: peach in most countries, PLUM in Syria/Lebanon/Jordan (their peach is durraaq); the book's Palestinian authors gloss it as peach.
- **Oral assessments**: Shami track only; always answer in full sentences.
