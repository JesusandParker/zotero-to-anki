## Letters

- **ء**: HAMZA -- the one new letter (3962-4190, then LE1-3 4190-4610). A consonant = glottal stop ('the glottal stop'). Never on the line at the START of a word; always needs a seat: alif with fatHa (أَ 'a'), Damma (أُ 'u'), kasra UNDER the alif (إِ 'i'), or alif with no vowel (أ, sukuun sound -- 'usually we don't write the sukuun at the top of hamza'); in the middle on waaw (ؤ, 'think of u') or on a dotless yaa' tooth (ئـ, 'think of i sound but don't write the dots'); on the line by itself only at the END of a word (شيء). At the start of a word it is 'sometimes written, sometimes not, but you still have to pronounce it -- we cannot start a word with a vowel'; follow the tashkiil. Exception: al- never takes a hamza. The spelling rule is 300-level. Board frames t4125/t4143 show all the seats.
- **ا**: Seat for initial hamza (alif-hamza); 'Alif at the beginning of a word is always a seat for hamza' (book) -- she showed أَ أُ إِ أ on the board. Second alif in akhawaat is the long vowel because it follows another vowel letter (4230-4267).
- **و**: Hamza seat ؤ ('think of u'); in akhawaat the waaw is a consonant w because two vowel letters come together.
- **ي**: Hamza seat: medial tooth WITHOUT the two dots (ئـ); final ئ on an 'independent yaa''. 'When you see this letter, think of [i] sound, but don't write the dots' (4106-4114).
- **ب**: Only incidental: spotted on the ketchup bag (116); ab = alif-hamza-fatHa + baa' (4283-4289).
- **ت**: Shaami replaces thaa' with taa' (tneen, tlaate, tmaane).
- **ث**: 'We're lazy about the thaa'' -- in Shaami it becomes taa' (1804-1808, 2028-2031, 2850-2851). thalaatha, thamaaniya, athaath, ithbaat.
- **ج**: Only in the writing-quiz aside ('don't put it on the jiim', 707).
- **ح**: 'The H is capital H, that means it's a Haa'' -- waaHid is NOT waahid (1715-1724). ibHaar.
- **خ**: No explanation today; heard in khamsa, akh, ukht, akhawaat, ukhrija.
- **د ذ ر ز**: Not taught on 9/22. She said she'd introduce two letters on Thursday 9/24 (1224-1231); the Unit 4 homework drills contain them.
- **ع (outside Units 1-4)**: Exaggerate it in arbaca so you'll spell it right later; 'almost in every number'.
- **ل (outside Units 1-4)**: Arabic L is always light -- not the dark English L of 'falafel' (1985-2020).
- **س (outside Units 1-4)**: khamsa has an S (siin), not a hamza: 'make sure it's not [khamza], it's an S' (3517-3521).
